Lipella Pharmaceuticals Inc.

Lipella Pharmaceuticals Inc. developed novel therapies using proprietary liposomal drug delivery technology and advanced multiple drug candidates into FDA-authorized clinical trials. Lipella completed its initial public offering in 2022, and its common stock began trading on the Nasdaq Capital Market. The Company reported Phase 2 results in hemorrhagic cystitis and oral lichen planus.

Following financing difficulties in 2025, Lipella filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection in March 2026. In June 2026, the Company completed a court-approved sale of its clinical development programs, intellectual property, and regulatory assets to XRAIY LLC. The acquired programs are now being pursued under the Kube Biosciences name.
